What this demo does NOT do reliably
  • Compare-across-periods clarify flow. "compared to last year" needs a metric AND survives the Q clarify, but the compare marker itself is not re-checked on the follow-up turn.
  • Forecasting. "Predict my revenue" refuses (Route C). No time-series model.
  • Cross-seller benchmarks. "compare to other sellers" refuses (Route R). No cohort math.
  • Route B needs a live LLM key. The B chips (bicycle helmet, candle) send one small ~290-token call to the model. Without a connected key, both chips still work but the pipeline falls to C with an honest "I could not identify the term" refusal (no number invented). With a key: bicycle helmet resolves to sports_leisure and answers 105 orders; candle stays refused because no category fits.
  • Non-English input. Vocabulary and phrase lists are English only.
